Bridging the Digital Divide Through Practical Skills

At Isbat University, we believe technology should be accessible to everyone.

Located in Kampala,we provide modern computer labs, experienced instructors, and industry-relevant training for students, job seekers, and corporate teams.

Join a supportive learning community dedicated to helping you build confidence and succeed in today's digital economy.

Our Mission

To empower individuals with the digital skills they need to thrive in the modern economy.

We are committed to providing high-quality, accessible technology education that bridges the gap between traditional learning and contemporary industry demands.
